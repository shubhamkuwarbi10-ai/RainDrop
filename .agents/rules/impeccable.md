# Impeccable Design & UI Craftsmanship Rule

Always adhere to the Impeccable standards whenever designing, building, modifying, or reviewing frontend components, user interfaces, or styling:

1. **Visual Distinction & Hierarchy**: Ensure immediate visual clarity with distinct typographic weight, proportional line heights, and deliberate focal points.
2. **Spatial Harmony (8pt Grid)**: Use consistent padding, margin, and gap scales (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64px). Ensure interactive targets are at least 40×40px.
3. **Curated Color & High Contrast**: Use tailored, rich palettes with accessible contrast (WCAG AAA/AA). Avoid raw primary colors and pitch black backgrounds.
4. **Depth & Translucency**: Use layered soft shadows, refined borders (`border-white/10` / `border-slate-200/80`), and frosted glassmorphism with high legibility.
5. **Micro-Interactions**: Implement snappy, GPU-accelerated hover and active feedback (transform, opacity) with natural easing.
6. **Zero Layout Shifts**: Prevent content jumping by explicitly defining aspect ratios and dimensional constraints.
7. **Authentic Plain Copy**: Use clear, everyday layman explanations focusing on real user problems and practical solutions instead of artificial or overly academic jargon.
