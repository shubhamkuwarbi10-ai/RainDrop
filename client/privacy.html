Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026 · Last Updated: 2026-09-18

1. Overview & Commitment

RainDrop ("we", "our", or "the Platform") provides municipal flood nowcasting, hydrological telemetry visualization, and evacuation dispatch services. We are strictly committed to data minimization, transparency, and preserving user privacy.

2. Data We Collect

  • Spatial Telemetry & Sensor Data: River levels, automated weather station (AWS) rainfall readings, and stormwater pump status. None of these contain personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Ephemeral Geolocation: If enabled for route emergency navigation, device coordinates are processed strictly in-memory client-side and never persisted.
  • Technical Analytics: Anonymized performance metrics, response latencies, and error diagnostics to ensure system reliability during extreme monsoon events.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use lightweight local storage tokens solely to persist user workspace preferences (such as selected GIS basemap layers and unit toggles) and to record cookie consent choices.

4. Third-Party Services & GIS Providers

Basemap tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap and ESRI World Imagery under their respective public open-access licenses. No tracking cookies are transmitted to third-party ad networks.

5. Security & Ingestion Safeguards

All client-server communications are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. All backend secrets and database credentials are strictly isolated from client-facing bundles.

6. Contact & Data Officer

For inquiries regarding telemetry data, GIS API compliance, or data subject requests, please contact our Municipal Emergency Operations Desk at privacy@raindrop.gis.local.
