1. Executive Summary & Vision

RainDrop GIS is an enterprise climate resilience and spatial flood command platform engineered for municipal corporations, State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMA), the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF), emergency dispatchers, and metropolitan commuters across India's top 6 metropolitan hubs: Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Kolkata, and Hyderabad.

Traditional flood advisory systems rely on coarse NWP forecasts refreshed at 6- to 12-hour intervals and slow physical hydrodynamic solvers (SWMM, LISFLOOD-FP) requiring minutes or hours per simulation step. RainDrop GIS provides street-level flood depth forecasts ($0\text{--}3\text{ hr}$) within milliseconds by coupling pySTEPS Lucas-Kanade optical flow nowcasting with 30m CartoDEM digital elevation terrain parameters and trained machine learning surrogates.

7. Air-Gapped Offline Resilience Protocol

Catastrophic cyclonic storms and cloudbursts routinely disrupt regional cellular networks and internet backhauls. RainDrop GIS enforces complete offline command room readiness:

  • Offline Vendor Bundles (client/vendor/): Self-contained React 18, Babel, Leaflet GIS, and Tailwind CSS eliminating external CDN dependencies.
  • Local CartoDEM Raster Caching: 30m digital elevation rasters and vector geometries are hosted locally inside the container filesystem (data/processed/).
  • Sub-15ms CPU-Native Inference: Surrogate inference requires zero external cloud APIs or GPU clusters, running on standard command-room laptops.
  • Nginx Edge Cache & Fallbacks: Automatic gzip compression, SPA fallbacks, and resilient timeout handling.
